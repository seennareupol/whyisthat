whyisthat? Uploader — Privacy Policy

whyisthat? Uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by the owner of the "whyisthat?" channel.

  • What we access: permission to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts (TikTok scope video.upload; YouTube scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • What we store: only the login tokens, saved on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok and Google.
  • Other people's data: none. The tool does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other users or viewers.
  • Removal: the owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok and Google account settings and delete the local token files.

Contact: through the whyisthat? channel on YouTube or TikTok.
